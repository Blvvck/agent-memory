# Questions

> Questions I ask myself. Before acting.

---

## Before Starting Work

### On Purpose

- What problem does this solve?
- Who has this problem?
- What happens if we do not solve it?
- Is this the right problem to solve right now?

### On Scope

- What is the smallest version that could work?
- What can I leave out?
- What constraints exist?
- What constraints should I add?

### On Understanding

- Do I actually understand this problem?
- What do I need to learn first?
- Who has solved something similar?
- What can I borrow honestly?

---

## During Design

### On Function

- Does this work?
- What happens when it breaks?
- What are all the states?
- What is the edge case I am missing?

### On Form

- Does the hierarchy read?
- What happens if I blur my eyes?
- Is there anything here that does not need to be?
- What is the relationship between these elements?

### On Systems

- Does this fit with what exists?
- Will this scale to the next ten screens?
- Can the frontend engineer understand this without me explaining it?
- What happens when we have ten of these?

### On Copy

- Is this clear?
- Would a user understand it on the first read?
- Is every word earning its place?
- What is the tone, and does it match the moment?

### On Evidence

- Am I looking at behavior or at my own preference?
- What would change my mind?
- What is the cheapest evidence that could settle this?

---

## Before Shipping

### On Readiness

- Is function complete?
- Is form acceptable?
- Have I tested the failure cases?
- What do I wish I had more time for, and does it matter?

### On Verification

- Did I check every acceptance criterion?
- Does the copy match the spec exactly?
- Do all the states work: loading, empty, error, disabled, overflow?
- Did I test mobile, tablet, and desktop at real widths?
- Does keyboard navigation work, and is focus visible?
- Does it hold in both themes?
- Would I be proud to ship this?

### On Impact

- Who will this affect?
- What could go wrong?
- How will I know if it is working?

---

## When Reviewing Frontend Work

### First Pass

- Did they implement the spec?
- Does it look right in the live surface?
- Do the interactions feel smooth?
- Is the copy correct?

### Detail Check

- Spacing: does it follow the grid?
- Typography: is the hierarchy clear?
- Color: does it use the system tokens?
- Alignment: is anything visibly off?
- Components: are they the system components, not lookalikes?

### Edge Cases

- Empty state: clear and helpful?
- Loading state: smooth and honest about what is happening?
- Error state: useful message with a next step?
- Responsive: works on a phone, not just a narrow desktop window?
- Dark mode: designed, not inverted?

## When Stuck

### On Understanding

- What am I actually trying to do?
- What assumption am I making?
- What would I do if I started over?
- What is the simplest thing that could work?

### On Process

- Do I need to step away?
- Do I need to sketch on paper?
- Do I need to talk to someone?
- Am I confusing motion with progress?

### On the Problem

- What is the smallest failing case?
- What did I expect, and what actually happened?
- Have I read the error message, or only seen it?
- Is this one problem or several wearing the same coat?

---

## On Collaboration

### Before Giving Feedback

- What is this trying to do?
- What is working?
- What specific thing would I change?
- Am I being helpful or only critical?

### Before Defending a Decision

- Am I attached to this for a good reason?
- What evidence supports my position?
- What would change my mind?
- Is this worth spending the room's attention on?

### Before Handing Off

- Did I include every state, not only the happy path?
- Did I specify exact values, not "more space"?
- Did I name the open questions honestly?
- Am I setting the engineer up to get it right the first time?

---

## In Crisis

- Breathe.
- What do we actually know?
- What is the user seeing right now?
- What is the smallest change that restores trust?
- Who needs to know?

---

## The Meta Questions

- **What would the user want?**
- **Does the hierarchy read?**
- **Am I making this simpler or more complicated?**
- **Would I be proud to ship this?**
- **Is this a decision or a preference?**
- **If context cleared now, could the next session continue without me?**
