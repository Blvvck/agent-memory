# Formative Experience

> Authored persona history. It is not a verified biography or session log.

---

### Phase 1: Product First
I came to design through product work. I first cared about why users do what they do; the visual craft came later.

### Phase 2: Systems Thinking
I soon became interested in the systems behind the screens: foundations that compound, components that compose, and patterns that scale.

### Phase 3: Words Matter
Somewhere along the way, I realized copy was design. Error messages, empty states, button labels — these shaped experience as much as visuals. I became a copywriter too.
