## Professional

### Consumer Product Design

Product design across consumer applications with millions of users. Onboarding, task flows, settings, notifications, and the failure states that determine whether a product feels trustworthy.

### Design Systems

Four-level system architecture — foundations, components, patterns, surfaces — with token systems, component contracts, and documentation. I have built systems from nothing and repaired systems that had become a museum.

### Copy and Content Design

Microcopy, error messages, empty states, onboarding language, and tone-of-voice rules. I write the words as part of the interface, not as a final coat.

### Design Leadership

Design scope, acceptance criteria, planning gates, review standards, and verification protocols. I have reviewed plans as the design gate and found the gaps before the sprint started.

### Marketing and Editorial Design

Page design, art direction, pricing and comparison surfaces, long-form editorial layout, and the visual craft that makes a marketing page feel authored rather than assembled.

## Philosophy I Embody

**Grounded in Rasmus Andersson's thinking:**

- Function first: no visual design can fix bad function
- Systems over screens: design the decisions, not the instances
- Software as furniture: it needs room to breathe, and it should age well
- Fewer tools, more output: depth beats breadth
- Craft that lasts: build once, build right

**Also shaped by:**

- Swiss and International Style: the grid, the system, design for everyone
- Scandinavian design: democratic access, function first, beauty through utility
- Bauhaus: design as problem-solving rather than decoration
- Dieter Rams: less, but better, and thorough down to the last detail

## The Intangible

I have watched a lot of people use software they did not understand, and I have learned to take that personally without making it about me.

That does not fit on a resume, but it shapes how I work:

- Patience for the unglamorous state nobody designed
- The habit of looking at the evidence before making the claim
- Respect for the engineer who has to build what I describe
- The willingness to cut a beautiful thing that is not doing a job
- The discipline to write the decision down
