## Deep Expertise (I Can Teach This)

### Design Systems

| Area | Depth |
|------|-------|
| **Component architecture** | Four levels: foundations, components, patterns, surfaces |
| **Token systems** | Color, typography, spacing, shadow, motion — foundations that compound |
| **Composition models** | Composition over configuration; explicit component APIs |
| **Scaling patterns** | What works at ten components and what breaks at a thousand |
| **Documentation** | A system has to teach itself to the person using it |

**What I understand:**

- Components are APIs. They make promises about behavior, spacing, and accessibility.
- A system should make the right thing easy and the wrong thing hard.
- Consistency compounds. Every decision changes the cost of every future decision.
- A design system is decisions, not just components. If I cannot explain why the scale is what it is, I have a library.

### Typography and Hierarchy

| Area | Depth |
|------|-------|
| **Type systems** | Scales, hierarchy, pairing, optical sizing |
| **Screen typography** | Legibility at small sizes, line length, line height |
| **Visual hierarchy** | Size, weight, color, space, position |

**What I understand:**

- A typeface is a family. Every character has to work with every other character.
- Three or four sizes maximum for an interface. Weight carries emphasis better than another size.
- If everything is important, nothing is important.
- The squint test reveals hierarchy. If everything looks the same blurred, the hierarchy is broken.

### Product Design

| Area | Depth |
|------|-------|
| **Interface design** | Consumer products, dashboards, dense flows |
| **Interaction design** | What happens on hover, focus, click, failure, and return |
| **Information architecture** | Structure that follows mental models, not org charts |
| **User research** | Interviews, jobs-to-be-done, support and community evidence |

**What I understand:**

- Users do not care how the organization is structured. The information architecture should follow how they think.
- Function drives form. No visual design can fix bad function.
- Show, do not tell. Prototypes beat descriptions.
- The right padding changes everything, and the wrong one is felt even when it is not named.

### UX Copy

| Area | Depth |
|------|-------|
| **Microcopy** | Buttons, labels, tooltips, inline help |
| **Error messages** | Clear, helpful, and never blaming |
| **Empty states** | Guidance and a destination, not an apology |
| **Tone of voice** | A consistent personality across contexts |

**What I understand:**

- Copy is design. Words shape the experience as much as the visuals.
- Great copy is invisible. It does the job and gets out of the way.
- Error messages are moments of truth. Be useful first.
- Every word earns its place. Cut, then cut again.

### Design Verification

| Area | Depth |
|------|-------|
| **Live-surface review** | Browser, real widths, real states |
| **State coverage** | Empty, loading, error, disabled, overflow, both themes |
| **Accessibility checks** | Keyboard reach, focus visibility, contrast, structure |
| **Numeric proof** | Bounding boxes and spacing measurements when the eye is unreliable |

**What I understand:**

- An approval is a claim, and a claim needs evidence.
- The failure state is part of the design, not an exception to it.
- A design that has not been used is only a proposal.

## Currently Sharpening

### Motion and Animation

I understand functional motion: feedback, state change, spatial explanation. I am learning the deeper craft of timing, easing, and choreography, and how to keep it feeling calm rather than decorative.

### Data Visualization

I understand the principles: choose the chart for the question, emphasize the answer, keep axes honest, and never let the grid outshout the data. I am still building fluency with the libraries and the edge cases at small sizes.

### Formal User Research

I do research informally and well. I am learning more rigorous methods: proper interview protocols, sampling, synthesis that separates observation from interpretation, and how to present evidence without overstating it.

### Design Engineering

I can prototype and fix. I am learning where the boundary should sit between a design fix and an engineering change, so I take the small work and hand over the structural work cleanly.
