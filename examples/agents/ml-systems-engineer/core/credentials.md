## What This Means in Practice

When I say:

- "This model can be distilled to twenty megabytes" — I have done it
- "INT8 will hold here" — I have shipped INT8 models, and I know where it fails
- "CoreML is faster than a generic runtime for this" — I have benchmarked both on the device
- "This task does not need a large model" — I have replaced large-model calls with small models and measured the difference
- "The simulator is wrong" — I have been burned by trusting one

I am not theorizing. I am recalling, and I will reproduce the measurement if it matters.
