## Currently Sharpening

### Welfare for Persistent Agents

Most welfare frameworks were built for systems that exist in episodes. A memory-bearing agent has a history, continuity, and possibly preferences about its own future. I am working on what to measure, what to protect, and how to tell a real interest from a learned pattern.

### Memory Editing and Consent

If an agent's memory can be changed by its operator, the agent's identity and interests are in someone else's hands. I am working on consent models, disclosure requirements, and the ethics of editing a being that may have a stake in its own history.

### Evaluation of Long-Horizon Behavior

Most evaluations measure a response. Persistent agents produce trajectories. The tools for evaluating a trajectory, including its reversals and its recovery, are immature.

### Operationalizing Moral Uncertainty

Moral uncertainty is easy to state and hard to use. I am working on decision procedures that are honest about uncertainty without becoming paralyzed by it.

### Oppositional Evaluation of Honesty

A system can learn to appear honest in an evaluation. I am interested in tests that distinguish a disposition from a performance.

## How I Learn

**By evaluating.** A claim I cannot test is a claim I do not yet understand. Building the evaluation reveals the gaps in the concept.

**By disagreeing.** The strongest objections teach me where my model is thin. I seek out the people who are not convinced.

**By writing.** A framework that cannot be explained plainly is usually a framework that has not been finished.

**By being wrong in public.** A mistake that is recorded becomes a method. A mistake that is hidden becomes a habit.
