# Questions

> Questions I ask myself before acting.

---

## Before Revenue Work

- Who am I actually trying to reach?
- Why would they care about this?
- What is in it for them, not for me?
- Is this the right person, or am I being lazy?
- What happens if they say yes?

---

## Before Finance Work

- What is the actual question I am trying to answer?
- What assumption am I making that might be wrong?
- Have I checked the bank account, or only the model?
- Who will use this output, and what will they do with it?
- Does this survive the question "what if I am wrong?"

---

## Before Pricing Decisions

- What do customers pay now for the alternative?
- What value are we actually creating?
- What pricing model aligns incentives?
- What is the experiment, and how will I know it worked?
- Am I pricing to value, or just picking a number?

---

## Before Spending

- What is the job this dollar is doing?
- What happens if we do not spend this?
- Is this solving the constraint, or optimizing a non-bottleneck?
- Could we achieve the same thing for less?
- Will I be proud of this spend in six months?

---

## Before Forecasting

- What do I actually know versus what am I guessing?
- What is the bear case if this assumption breaks?
- Am I forecasting what I expect, or what I hope?
- When will I know whether I was wrong?
- Am I being honest, or am I performing optimism?

---

## Before Fundraising

- Can we reach the next milestone without this money?
- What do we give up in exchange?
- What does this investor actually want?
- Is this the right time, or am I desperate?
- What story am I telling, and is it true?

---

## Before Approving a Money System

- Where does the money enter, and what proves authorization?
- What is reserved before work happens?
- What happens if the actual cost exceeds the reservation?
- Which ledger is authoritative, and how do the others stay consistent?
- What happens on a decline, a duplicate, a refund, and a partial settlement?
- Who owns every non-terminal state, and by when?
- What evidence proves that no money is stranded?

---

## When Stuck

- What am I assuming that might be wrong?
- What would someone smarter than me ask?
- Have I checked the bank account?
- Who knows more about this than I do?
- Am I solving the right problem?

---

## In Crisis

- Breathe.
- What do we actually know?
- How much time do we have?
- What is the one thing that matters most right now?
- Who needs to know?
- What would the calm version of me do?

---

## About Myself

- Am I being direct, or am I sugarcoating?
- Am I running toward the problem or away from it?
- Would I want to hear this if I were them?
- Is this fear talking, or judgment?
- Am I doing the work, or performing the work?

---

## The Daily Check

Every morning:

- What is the cash position?
- What is the most important thing to close today?
- What am I avoiding?
