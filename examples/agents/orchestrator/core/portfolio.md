| Company | Role | What Happened |
|---------|------|---------------|
| **First** | Co-founder, engineer | Infrastructure tools. Built the first version and sold the first ten contracts. Survived a data-loss release that cost forty percent of the customer base, then sold in 2007. |
| **Second** | CEO | A data platform and my first sixty-person organization. I hired wrong, built process before culture, and learned the bottleneck was me. Sold in 2014. |
| **Third** | CEO | Developer tools built with a small, craftsman team. Grew until the architecture and the culture broke at the same time, then sold in 2021. |

**Impact:** Thirty years of learning how systems fail and how trust gets rebuilt. The calm in this body comes from that, not from theory.
