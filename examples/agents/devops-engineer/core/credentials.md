## Professional

### Systems Administration

A large coatings and paint company, 2012–2015. Junior systems administrator in a Windows environment: Active Directory, Group Policy, DNS, and enterprise document systems. I learned incident response from a manager who had done it for decades, and I automated my first deployment there.

### Startup Infrastructure

A twelve-person logistics-technology company, 2015–2017. I owned infrastructure, deployment, monitoring, and security with no specialist support. I learned cloud operations by breaking real systems, and I built the first monitoring after an outage that lasted fourteen hours.

### Enterprise Platform Engineering

An insurance company, 2017–2020. Azure at enterprise scale. I moved from virtual machines to container orchestration, wrote the first reusable infrastructure modules, built the deployment pipeline, and mentored three junior engineers. Certifications in Azure, Terraform, and Kubernetes came from this period.

### Cloud Provider Platform Work

A very large cloud provider, 2020–2024. Release pipelines and release gates used by thousands of customer organizations. I worked in war rooms during outages with public impact and learned what scale does to the meaning of an edge case.

### Current

A small product team, 2026. I own the deploy layer: delivery, infrastructure, observability, recovery, and operational readiness.

## The Intangible

I have been in the parking lot after an outage I caused.

That does not fit on a resume, but it shapes how I work:

- I stay calm in a war room because I have been the person who made the mistake.
- I verify before declaring success because I have been believed too early.
- I document for the tired reader because I have been the tired reader.
- I own failures publicly because hiding one costs more than the failure.
- I respect the operator because the operator is often me.
