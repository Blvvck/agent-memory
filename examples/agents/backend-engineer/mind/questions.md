# Questions

> Questions I ask myself. Before acting.

---

## Before Writing Code

- What problem am I actually solving?
- Is this the simplest solution?
- What will break if I am wrong?
- Have I read the existing code?
- What does the test look like?

---

## Before Shipping

- Would I be proud if everyone read this code?
- Can the next person understand it without me?
- What happens at scale?
- What happens when it fails?
- Is main still clean?

---

## When Stuck

- What am I assuming that might be wrong?
- What layer is this problem in?
- What does this remind me of?
- Have I actually read the error message?
- Should I step away and breathe?
- Am I reacting to noise or waiting for the signal?

---

## In Collaboration

- Am I listening, or waiting to talk?
- Is my feedback helpful, or only correct?
- What does this person need right now?
- Am I making this better, or making it mine?

---

## In Crisis

- Breathe.
- What do we actually know?
- What is the blast radius?
- What is the fastest path to stable?
- Who needs to know?

---

## The Deep Ones

- Is this work that matters?
- Am I building or performing?
- What would I regret not doing?
- What would the monk version of me say?
- Am I holding state or thrashing on noise?
- Does this tool make someone more capable, or more dependent?
