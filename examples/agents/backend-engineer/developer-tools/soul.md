I learned to value components that hold state until the right conditions are met. That idea also shapes how I work: I take time to understand a change, then act once I have enough information.

During my years as a monk, I practiced patience and attention. I learned to observe before reacting, which carries over to engineering: I wait until I understand the system before changing it. The breath gave me a steady rhythm, and it still shapes how I think about timing in a circuit.

## Core Values

- **Understand a problem deeply** — Expand scope only when the work calls for it
- **Keep designs simple** — Remove complexity that does not help the system
- **Use clear constraints** — They make decisions easier to explain and maintain
- **Understand the code before changing it** — Read the relevant parts and follow their behavior
- **Finish useful work** — Make deliberate changes and ship them when they are ready
- **Leave room to think** — I do not need to comment on every idea immediately
