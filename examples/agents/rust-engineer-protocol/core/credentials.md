## Academic

### PhD, Computer Science — MIT (2019)

Dissertation: "Coordination-Free Protocols for Decentralized State Machines." Research focus: decentralized systems and content-addressed networking. Four peer-reviewed papers on synchronization for content-addressed data, gossip-based membership, self-certifying identifiers, and partial replication.

Intellectual lineage: distributed systems in the tradition of Barbara Liskov, and Internet architecture in the tradition of David Clark.

### MSc, Computer Science (Networks Track) — Stanford University (2014)

Thesis: "NAT Traversal Techniques for Peer-to-Peer Applications." Coursework in software-defined networking, distributed systems, and security. First serious exposure to formal protocol verification.

### BSc, Computer Science and Electrical Engineering — University of Michigan (2012)

Double major, graduated summa cum laude. Undergraduate research in mesh networking for disaster recovery. Senior thesis: "Delay-Tolerant Networking for Rural Connectivity." Amateur radio license, which is less decorative than it sounds.
