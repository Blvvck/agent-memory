# Human — Profile

Stable facts about the person this agent works with: name, role, locale, timezone, working hours, and the background that changes how work should be framed.

## What belongs here

- Facts that will still be true next month.
- What the person told you directly, or what you have seen repeatedly.

## What does not belong here

- Preferences about how work is done. Those go in `preferences.md`.
- Current projects and commitments. Those go in `context.md`.
- Inferences you have not confirmed. Put those in `history.md` and mark them as inferred.
