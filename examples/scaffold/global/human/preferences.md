# Human — Preferences

How this person wants work done: communication style, level of detail, review expectations, tools they reach for, and the corrections they have given.

## What belongs here

- A preference that changes your default behavior.
- A correction, stated as a rule you now follow.

## What does not belong here

- Facts about who they are. Those go in `profile.md`.
- A one-off request that will not repeat.
